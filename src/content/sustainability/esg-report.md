# Why Structural Safety Is Non-Negotiable in Indian Construction

India loses hundreds of lives every year to building collapses and structural failures. Most of these tragedies stem from poor material quality, inadequate soil testing, and the absence of stability certifications. At TSB Constructions, structural safety isn't a checkbox — it's our core expertise.

## The Reality of Construction in Rural Karnataka

In many parts of Karnataka — especially in tier-2 and tier-3 towns like Gauribidanur, Chintamani, and Sira — buildings are often constructed without proper structural assessments. Homeowners rely on local masons without engineering oversight, leading to:

- Uneven load distribution
- Weak foundations on expansive black cotton soil
- Corrosion of reinforcement steel due to poor cover
- Cracks and settlement within the first few years

## How TSB Ensures Structural Integrity

Our Managing Director, Er. Paramesh T B, is a qualified structural engineer who personally oversees the safety protocols on every project:

### NDT (Non-Destructive Testing)
We use advanced NDT methods including **Rebound Hammer Testing** and **Ultrasonic Pulse Velocity** to assess concrete strength without damaging the structure. This is critical for both new constructions and existing buildings that need stability audits.

### Stability Certification
TSB Constructions provides official **Structural Stability Certificates** — a requirement for building approvals, bank loans, and insurance in Karnataka. Our assessments cover:

- Foundation adequacy
- Load-bearing capacity
- Seismic resistance (important for Zone II & III regions in Karnataka)
- Overall structural health

### Quality Materials
We source only ISI-certified steel, tested concrete mixes, and verified aggregates. Every batch of materials undergoes quality checks before it reaches the site.
