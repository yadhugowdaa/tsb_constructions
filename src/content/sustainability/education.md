# From Classrooms to Construction Sites — Our Educational Mission

India produces over 1.5 million engineering graduates every year, yet the construction industry faces a persistent skills gap. At TSB Constructions, we believe the solution starts at the grassroots — right here in Karnataka.

## Our MoU with Shridevi Institute of Engineering & Technology

In a landmark step, TSB Construction & Developers signed a Memorandum of Understanding with **Shridevi Institute of Engineering & Technology, Tumkur**, one of the region's premier engineering colleges. This partnership is designed to bridge the gap between academic theory and real-world construction practice.

### What Students Gain

- **Live Site Visits**: Students visit our active project sites across Chickballapur and Gauribidanur, observing modern construction techniques firsthand.
- **Internship Programs**: Final-year civil engineering students are offered hands-on internships, working alongside our structural engineers on real projects.
- **Expert Lectures**: Our Managing Director, Er. Paramesh T B, regularly conducts guest lectures on advanced construction methods, NDT (Non-Destructive Testing), and structural stability analysis.

## Why This Matters for Karnataka

Karnataka's construction sector needs skilled professionals who understand both traditional building wisdom and modern engineering. The state's infrastructure push — from highway projects to affordable housing — demands engineers who have worked on actual sites, not just in simulation labs.

## Impact So Far

Since the inception of this partnership, multiple batches of students have completed site training programs. Several interns have gone on to join the construction industry with a level of practical confidence that sets them apart.

> *"When a student sees a building rise from the ground up, engineering stops being a subject and becomes a calling."*
> — Er. Paramesh T B, Managing Director, TSB Constructions
